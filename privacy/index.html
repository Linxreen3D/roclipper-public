RoClipper - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 October 2026

RoClipper runs locally on its operator's own computer. This policy explains what data it handles when the operator connects their social-media accounts.

1. Data RoClipper receives from platforms

When the operator connects an account (TikTok, Instagram or YouTube) through the platform's OAuth consent screen, RoClipper receives:

  • access and refresh tokens,
  • the account's basic profile information (user ID, username),
  • after publishing: post IDs and URLs, upload status and basic performance metrics (for example views), where the API provides them.

RoClipper only requests the permissions it needs to publish videos and read their status.

2. How the data is stored

Tokens and app credentials are stored only in the operator's local macOS Keychain. They are not written to the database, logs or web pages. Other data stays in a local database and on local or operator-owned storage on the same computer. RoClipper does not run a cloud backend.

3. How the data is used

The data is used only to upload the operator's videos to the operator's own accounts, to check whether an upload succeeded, and to show the operator their results. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising or profiling.

4. Data sent to platforms

Videos, captions and the related API requests are sent only to the platform the operator publishes to, through that platform's official API. Each platform processes them under its own privacy policy.

5. Retention and deletion

Removing an account in RoClipper deletes its stored tokens. Access can also be revoked at any time in the platform's settings. Video files are deleted automatically once they are no longer needed. The operator can delete all local data at any time.

6. Contact

RoClipper is operated privately by the owner of the connected accounts. Questions about this policy go to the operator through the contact details on their connected profile.

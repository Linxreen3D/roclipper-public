RoClipper - Terms of Service

Last updated: 7 October 2026

RoClipper is a private, self-hosted tool. Its operator runs it on their own computer to manage their own social-media accounts: it creates short video clips from content the operator is allowed to use and uploads them to the operator's own accounts through the platforms' official APIs (TikTok Content Posting API, Instagram API, YouTube Data API). It is not offered as a service to the public.

1. Use

Only the operator uses RoClipper, and only with accounts they own or are authorised to manage. Every account is connected with the platform's own OAuth login and consent screen. The operator can revoke that access at any time in RoClipper or in the platform's account settings.

2. Content and platform rules

The operator is responsible for having the rights to all published content and for following the terms, community guidelines and rate limits of each platform. RoClipper uses official APIs only. It does not bypass platform limits, automate logins or generate fake engagement.

3. No warranty

RoClipper is provided "as is", without warranty of any kind. Uploads can fail or be rejected by a platform, and the operator checks the results.
